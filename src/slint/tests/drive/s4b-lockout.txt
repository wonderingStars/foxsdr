# s4b-lockout (screen lane S4b, row G2-43 / SCANNER_EXTRAS 5): under the Scanner body the locked-out frequencies are LISTED (what the engine holds now: fact.scan.lockouts), and "Always skip these
# frequencies" sends the configured locked list to the engine (SCANNER_EXTRAS 5): typed in MHz, read back FROM THE ENGINE as Hz in fact.scan.locked. A bad entry is refused on the screen and the engine keeps its list.

+1 state s4b.fact.scan.locked == "145500000.000;118100000.000"

+1 key enter
+1 type "Stop MHz" "100.4"

+1 key enter

+1 key enter

+1 click "Stop scan"
