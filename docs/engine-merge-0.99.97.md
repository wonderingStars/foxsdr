## 5. Display off and the frame's kick: the decision

The branch's frame kicks the pass (`engine_.frameKick()`, the first act of every iteration of `run()`'s loop, docs/engine-stage4.md 6.9), and the pass's own timer is the floor beneath it. Master's 81346188 keeps the loop turning with the display off - the message pump, the interface's own work, the radio and decoders on their threads - and skips only the draw, the present and the screenshot read-back, pacing the loop with a 33 ms wait in place of the vertical sync. **Decision: the kick still fires, unchanged, at the top of every iteration, drawn or not.** Why that is right and not just convenient, from the contract:

  * Nothing in the kick's contract depends on a frame being DRAWN. It is "a pass a frame, plus the timer as the no-frames floor" (6.9); a frame that draws nothing is still a frame, and the loop is still the loop.
  * The kicks now arrive every ~33 ms, which is MORE than the 24 ms grace. So between kicks the pass runs on its own timer: it must keep its ~60 passes a second. That is the one thing the existing cases did not hold (K1 is kicks at 16.7 ms, K2 is no kicks at all). **Test K6 in tests/test_pass_control.cpp** holds it: kicks every `cascade::gui::kDisplayOffFrameMs` (33 ms, the constant itself) for 2 s: the pass runs 50 to 70 a second, no two starts are more than the grace plus a margin apart, most passes are the timer's, and a kick that lands is still served. Measured natively three times: 61.0, 61.5 and 60.0 passes a second, about half of them the timer's, the longest gap between two starts 24.6 to 24.7 ms. Break-it: the engine's grace tripled (the kind of change that "pause rendering" could be mistaken for): K6 goes red (30.5 passes a second, none by the timer), and so does K2.
  * It runs under ThreadSanitizer (ad hoc, tests/tsan.cmake does not select test_pass_control; 6.24): 4 runs, 0 reports, K6 green in all four.
  * The real binary: test_display_off_app (master's, unchanged) runs the real window with a real WM_POWERBROADCAST: off at frame 60, on at 140: one off line, one on line saying 80 frames were not drawn, no screenshot read back while off, the run finishing all 200 frames.
