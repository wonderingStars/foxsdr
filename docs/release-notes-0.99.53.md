**FoxSDR from the Microsoft Store now tells you when the Store has an update.** A copy installed from the Microsoft Store never checked for updates itself - the Store was meant to deliver them - but the Store's own background updating can take a day or more, and until then you were not told anything. Now, once per launch, FoxSDR asks the Microsoft Store whether it has a newer version of your copy. When it does, a banner at the top of the menu says so, with an **Install from the Microsoft Store** key: Windows then asks you to confirm, downloads and installs the update, and FoxSDR may close while it does. Nothing is installed unless you press the key and confirm, and the question goes only to Microsoft's Store service - never to foxsdr.com, and nothing of ours is sent. You can turn it off under **Settings > Updates > Ask the Microsoft Store for updates at startup**. Copies installed from foxsdr.com are unchanged: they still ask foxsdr.com, as before.
