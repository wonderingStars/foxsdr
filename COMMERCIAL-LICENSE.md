**FoxSDR** is free — permanently — for noncommercial use under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). If you are a hobbyist, that is
the end of the matter: there is nothing to buy, nothing to register, no key, no
trial period, and no feature is held back from you.

**Any commercial use requires a separate paid licence.** That is where this
project earns its keep.

## Which one applies to me?

You are covered by the free noncommercial licence if you are:

- an individual using it for a hobby, personal study, experimentation or
  private entertainment;
- an amateur radio operator, SWL or enthusiast;
- a charity, school, university, public research body, public safety or
  health organisation, environmental organisation, or government institution
  (regardless of how you are funded).

You need a **commercial licence** if you are, for example:

- using it in the course of a business, including purely internally;
- selling it, or selling a product or device that includes it;
- bundling it with hardware you sell;
- using it to deliver a paid service to customers;
- building a product derived from it that you intend to sell.

If you are unsure, ask. Genuine hobby use is meant to be free, and asking costs
nothing. We would far rather answer a question than have anyone quietly
overstep.

## What a commercial licence gives you

- The right to use, modify and distribute the software commercially, within the
  scope of the tier you hold.
- All updates released during your licence term.
- Email support, with a target first response of two working days.
- For OEM and Redistribution licensees: input into the roadmap, and reasonable
  notice before anything that would break a shipped integration.

## What happens if I stop paying?

You keep what you already had. On non-renewal, your right to use and — for the
OEM and Redistribution tiers — to keep distributing the versions you received
during your paid term continues **perpetually**. You simply stop receiving new
versions and support.

This matters if you ship hardware: you cannot recall units already in
customers' hands, so a licence that evaporates on non-renewal would be one no
manufacturer could reasonably sign. Ours does not.

## Can I modify it?

Yes, under either licence. The noncommercial licence expressly permits changes
and new works — but a modified version is still bound by the same terms, so it
remains noncommercial-only unless you hold a commercial licence. You must also
pass on the licence terms and the `Required Notice:` line to anyone you give a
copy to.

  licences and are unaffected by the terms above. They may be used commercially
  in their own right; see `installer/THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt`, which is also
  installed alongside the application.
- **The plugin interface** (`src/core/plugin_abi.h`) is MIT-licensed on
  purpose, so anyone — including commercial vendors — can write plugins for
  this application without needing a licence from us.
- **Plugins are not covered by this licence.** Each plugin carries its own
  terms, stated in the plugin catalogue and displayed in the application before
  installation.
- **We do not police hobbyists.** There is no telemetry, no activation, and no
  licence check in the application. The commercial tiers work on
  self-certification, the same way every source-available business does.
