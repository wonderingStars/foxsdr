If you are unsure, ask. Genuine hobby use is meant to be free, and asking
costs nothing.

## What a commercial licence gives you

- The right to use, modify and distribute the software commercially.
- Terms negotiated to fit what you are actually doing (internal use, an
  embedded product, an OEM bundle, redistribution).
- Optional: priority support, and input into the roadmap.

## Can I modify it?

Yes, under either licence. The noncommercial licence explicitly permits
changes and new works — but a modified version is still bound by the same
terms, so it remains noncommercial-only unless you hold a commercial licence.
You must also pass on the licence terms and the `Required Notice:` line to
anyone you give a copy to.
